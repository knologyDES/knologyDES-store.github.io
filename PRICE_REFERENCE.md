# Price reference notes

The storefront uses fixed USD retail prices selected to sit within current U.S. gym-apparel market ranges as of September 2026.

Examples checked during the update included Gymshark Arrival 5-inch Shorts at $26, Gymshark Everyday Seamless Leggings at $38, Gymshark Everyday Seamless Racerback Sports Bra at $26, Gymshark Crew Socks around $16-$20, Nike Club caps around $28, and adidas Dropset training shoes around $90-$150 depending on model.

These knologyDES prices are store prices for the mockup, not official prices of Nike, adidas, or Gymshark products.
